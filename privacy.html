Clay Mob Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Clay Mob is a game for iPhone. This page explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: Clay Mob does not collect any personal data.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, upgrades, cards, buildings and settings) is saved on your iPhone only. A small play log (levels played, wins, times) is also kept on your iPhone to help balance the game. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

What we don't do

There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics services and no tracking. Clay Mob does not use your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.

Purchases

In-app purchases go through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; Clay Mob never sees your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy covers those transactions.

Children

Clay Mob collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it saved on your iPhone. You can also start over at any time from Settings > Reset progress.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
