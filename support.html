Clay Mob Support

Need a hand? Email carl_lightbourne@yahoo.com and tell us your iPhone model and what happened. We read every message.

My purchase didn't arrive

Close and reopen Clay Mob while connected to the internet; a purchase that didn't finish is delivered on the next launch. If it still isn't there, email us with the date of the purchase.

I got a new iPhone

The Extra Builder comes back with Settings > Restore Purchases. Coins, card packs, energy and the Starter Pack are used up when you buy them, so they can't be restored. Game progress is saved on the device it was played on.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can ask Apple for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

Clay Mob doesn't collect personal data. See the privacy policy.
